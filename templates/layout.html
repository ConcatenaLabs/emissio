Invite friends with a post

Put your referral link in a post, with your own words around it: what you tried, what you liked, what you would build. Anyone who joins through the link and earns {{seq .Threshold}} SEQ from tasks gets {{seq .Bonus}} SEQ, and so do you once you have earned {{seq .Threshold}} too. Then they can invite their own friends the same way.

The buttons write only your link, and on X your account code {{.Code}} under it: the words are yours. Posts that read alike from many accounts look like coordinated promotion to X and to Reddit, and can get your account limited and the link filtered.

{{if .QuoteURL}}

Quote Sequentia's official post on X. It introduces Sequentia and Emissio, so your followers see what it is about under your own words. A quote is a repost with your text on top; a plain repost carries no text, so it brings you no referrals.

  1. Press Quote the official post on X. X opens with your link and code, and Sequentia's post attached under them. Write your own words above them and press Post.
  2. Or by hand: open the official post, press the repost icon (the two arrows), choose Quote, paste your link and code (), write your own words and press Post.

What the buttons write, and where to post

On Reddit the button opens a text post with your link in it and nothing else: the title and the words are yours. Post it once, on your own profile or in one community whose rules allow referral links. The same post in several communities is what Reddit removes as spam. A post does not verify your Reddit account: your account page says how that is done.

On Telegram the button sends your link and one line to the chat you choose.

You do not have to explain everything. Whoever opens your link is told what Sequentia and Emissio are, that there is nothing to buy or deposit and that nobody asks for keys, before the form asks for anything. Post where sharing a link is welcome: your own profile or timeline, or a community whose rules allow it.
